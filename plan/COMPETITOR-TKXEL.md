# Competitor teardown: tkxel.com — future-phase ideas for JezeroX

Notes from studying https://tkxel.com (an 18-year, 800-person AI-native software agency —
where Usama worked). They are far bigger than JezeroX, so we don't copy scale; we borrow the
*structure and trust mechanics* that a lean, senior-led studio can execute credibly.

## What tkxel does well (and what we can adapt)

| tkxel pattern | What it does for them | JezeroX future phase |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-native positioning in the hero** | AI is the headline, not buried | ✅ Already done (Aurora hero leads with AI) |
| **Outcome-driven case-study headlines** ("35% fewer missed opportunities") | Concrete, scannable proof | Phase 2: add a real metric to each case study as we get client sign-off |
| **Case studies tagged by industry AND service, filterable** | Multiple discovery paths, SEO | ✅ Category filter shipped; add **industry** tags + filter next |
| **Engagement-models section** (dedicated team / offshore / fixed-price) | Lets buyers self-select | Phase 2: add "How we work together" — Fixed scope · Retainer · Fractional/consulting |
| **Resources hub** (blog, white papers, webinars, podcasts, newsroom) | Thought leadership + huge organic SEO surface | Phase 3: grow `/insights` into a real blog; add 1–2 downloadable guides (lead magnets) |
| **Lead-magnet white paper in hero** ("C-suite guide to AI") | Captures emails, nurtures | Phase 3: "AI automation checklist" or "n8n playbook" PDF behind an email opt-in |
| **Social-proof metrics** (years, team, retention %) | Credibility beyond logos | ✅ Using real Upwork proof; add a client logo strip once we have permission |
| **Video testimonials** (play buttons on quotes) | Motion + authenticity | Phase 3: record 1–2 short client video testimonials |
| **Recognition badges** (Inc. 5000, certs, tech partners) | Trust signals | Phase 2: add "Top Rated on Upwork" badge asset; pursue Vercel/n8n/OpenAI partner badges |
| **Granular mega-menu** (6 categories, 30+ sub-services) | Buyers self-serve without overwhelm | Not yet — we're intentionally lean. Revisit only if service count grows |
| **Industry landing pages** | Ranks for "[industry] + automation" | Phase 2 (high value): dedicated pages per target niche (see below) |

## Where JezeroX already WINS vs a big agency
- **Direct access to a senior engineer** — no account managers, no juniors. Lead into this hard.
- **Honest, verifiable proof** (real Upwork profile, real reviews) vs anonymized enterprise logos.
- **Faster, cheaper for SMBs** — tkxel targets enterprise; we own the SMB / founder / lean-team lane.

## Recommended future phases (priority order)

**Phase 2 — Conversion & SEO depth**
1. **Industry landing pages** for the highest-intent US niches: real estate, e-commerce, healthcare/dental, legal, financial services, marketing agencies, recruiting. Each: the industry's specific pain → the exact automations we build → a relevant case → CTA. (Big SEO win — ranks for "AI automation for [industry]".)
2. **Industry + service tags & filtering** on `/work`.
3. **Engagement models** section (Fixed scope · Retainer · Fractional AI engineer).
4. **Real metrics** on case studies (as clients approve).
5. **"Top Rated on Upwork" badge** + a client-logo strip (with permission).

**Phase 3 — Authority & lead capture**
6. Grow **/insights into a real blog** (2–4 posts/month on n8n, RAG, AI ops) — the biggest long-term organic channel.
7. **Lead magnet** (downloadable playbook/checklist) behind an email opt-in + a simple nurture sequence.
8. **1–2 video testimonials**.
9. **Pricing guidance** page or "starting from" ranges to pre-qualify leads.

**Phase 4 — Polish & scale**
10. Interactive **ROI / automation savings calculator** (great shareable lead tool).
11. Partner/certification badges (Vercel, n8n, OpenAI, AWS).
12. Multi-language or region targeting if we expand beyond US.

## Anti-patterns to avoid (don't copy tkxel here)
- Don't fake enterprise scale or invent metrics — our edge is honesty.
- Don't build a 30-item mega-menu for a solo studio; keep navigation lean.
- Don't gate everything; keep the core value visible.
